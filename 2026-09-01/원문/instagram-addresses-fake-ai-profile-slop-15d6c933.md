Instagram is finally taking steps to address the rise of fake AI-influencer accounts that have gotten harder to spot . It’s also renaming the “AI creator” label to “AI-generated profile” to make it clear when a profile features an AI-generated person that’s not a real human being.
 “We’ve heard that people don’t like seeing a profile that seems human, only to find out later that the person featured is AI-generated,” said Instagram in a blog post . “They want to know when a profile features an AI-generated person.”
 A look at how Instagram will deal with accounts that don’t self-label their AI-generated profiles. Image: Instagram To that end, Instagram will be hunting down accounts that don’t self-label their AI-generated profiles, and penalize them by limiting reach. That means their posts won’t be recommended to non-followers in Reels and Explore. Profile owners can appeal the decision if wrongly flagged.
